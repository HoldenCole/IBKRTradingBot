# The swing technique through real 3x sector funds, and a mirror short through inverse 3x funds: pre-registration (entry 116)

Written 2026-11-27 before any fund's returns were looked at. Nothing below may change after results are seen.

User direction: check 3x funds like SOXL, and inverse funds. Entry 115 found the swing rules transfer to SMH, XLK, XLY,
EEM, XLP, and to XLE, XLF and XLI with a trend gate. Those eight were picked by entry 115 over 1999-2026, so a
universe built from them is partly in-sample; the checks below say so where it matters.

## Part A. Inventory (descriptive)

For each candidate 3x bull and bear fund on a sector, industry or country (SOXL/SOXS, TECL/TECS, FAS/FAZ, ERX/ERY,
EDC/EDZ, WANT, DUSL, CURE, UTSL, DRN/DRV, NAIL, LABU/LABD, DPST, RETL, NUGT/DUST, GUSH/DRIP, YINN/YANG): first price date,
median daily dollar volume over the last 6 months, and the fund's actual daily return against 3x its underlying
1x ETF less the entry 95 costs (the tracking gap a year). A fund is usable if it has at least 5 years of history and
a median daily dollar volume of at least $5 million. Broad US market funds (UPRO/SPXU, TQQQ/SQQQ, UDOW/SDOW, TNA/TZA)
are not candidates here; the standing rule bars shorting the broad US market, and the long ones are the baseline.

## Part B. Long: the swing rules through the real 3x funds

Signals on the underlying 1x ETF exactly as in the live plan (RSI(2) < 10 and (below the lower Bollinger band or above
the 200-day); sell at the first close above the 5-day average or after 10 days); returns are the actual 3x fund's
close to close; 9 bp a side; idle cash earns nothing.

- B1 per fund, one position, the whole account, no gate and the live plan's regime gate: trades, mean, t, CAGR,
  maximum drawdown, from the fund's start.
- B2 the live plan with its universe widened to SPY, QQQ, SMH, XLK, XLY, EEM (the four entry 115 passers that have a
  usable 3x fund and passed without a trend gate), two slots, ranked by RSI(2), same gate, synthetic 3x as in entry
  115, eras A 1999-2006, B 2007-2016, C 2017-2026. B2 is an improvement only if it raises CAGR without a worse maximum
  drawdown in all three eras; because the four were chosen with 1999-2026 data, a pass is a lead to be paper-logged,
  not a change.

## Part C. Short: the mirror rule through inverse 3x funds

Mirror signal on the underlying: RSI(2) > 90 and (above the upper Bollinger(20, 2) band or below the 200-day
average); cover at the first close below the 5-day average or after 10 days. Two versions: no gate, and only when the
underlying's own month-end close is below its 10-month average (known at the prior month-end). Instruments: the
underlyings of the usable bear funds from part A. Per trade at 1x on the underlying (short, 5 bp a side): an
instrument passes if the mean is above zero with t >= 2 and positive in both halves (1999-2012 and 2013-2026, or the
underlying's start to 2012). For passers, the same trades through the actual inverse fund are reported.
