# Independent-data tests of the leads: pre-registration (entry 107)

Written 2026-11-27 before any result. Nothing below may change after results are seen.

User question: why have the leads not been fully tested? They were found or designed on the data that now
flatters them. This entry tests each one, with its rules frozen, on data it was not built from.

## A. The bitcoin 10-month rule (entry 104 lead; half of the entry 105 combination)

Rule, frozen: at a month-end close, hold the coin for the next month if the close is above the average of the
last 10 month-end closes; otherwise cash. 0.25% a side per switch. Compared with holding the coin.

- A1 other coins: ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE, BNB, ADA, LINK, AVAX, LTC (Binance spot daily closes, each from its first
  month plus 10). Passes if the rule beats holding on both CAGR and maximum drawdown in at least 6 of the 9.
- A2 neighbours on bitcoin (Coinbase, 2016-06 to the end): averages of 6, 8, 9, 11 and 12 month-ends. Passes if
  every neighbour beats holding on both CAGR and maximum drawdown, in both 2016-20 and 2021-26.
- A3 timing on bitcoin: the same rule checked on the 10th and on the 20th of each month (the average of the last
  10 such closes). Passes if both beat holding on CAGR and maximum drawdown over 2016-06 to the end.

The rule is confirmed if A1, A2 and A3 all pass.

## B. The 30% stop monthly Quadrant Balances bet (entry 99, design A, 30% stop, R2 re-entry)

Rule, frozen as in entry 99: all in at 3x when the monthly trend turns up below zero; held until the monthly trend
crosses below zero; trailing stop 30% on the position's closes; after a stop, re-enter at a month-end close above
the 10-month average while the window is open. Price-return indices (no dividends), 3x less 2 x (T-bill + 1%) and
0.95% a year, 9 bp a side. Compared with 3x buy and hold and with the same design without the stop.

Indices (Yahoo, each from its first month with 40 months of history for the trend oscillator): Nasdaq-100 (^NDX),
Russell 2000 (^RUT), Dow Jones Industrial (^DJI), Nikkei 225 (^N225), DAX (^GDAXI), FTSE 100 (^FTSE).

Passes if, on at least 4 of the 6, it beats 3x buy and hold on both CAGR and maximum drawdown and has a shallower
maximum drawdown than the no-stop version.
